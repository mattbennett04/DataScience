## Description

The series behaves like a random walk with upward drift. It stays near 4,000 for the first 1,500 days, then climbs to about 19,000 by t ≈ 3600. At t = 3626 it collapses to about 4,500 in a single step. That jump is about 60 times the standard deviation of the daily changes. After the collapse, the series continues at the lower level with small fluctuations.

This dataset tests how each network handles a sudden, permanent regime change.
