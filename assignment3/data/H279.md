# H279

- **Source:** M4 forecasting competition, series `H279`
- **Frequency:** Hourly
- **Domain:** Other
- **Start date (from M4-info):** 07-06-10 12:00
- **Length:** 1008 points (960 from the M4 train file, 48 from the M4 test file)
- **Stationarity:** Mostly stationary. The ADF test rejects a unit root (p < 0.001), but the KPSS test rejects level stationarity (p = 0.01).

## Description

A smooth, regular 24-hour cycle with values roughly between 10 and 22. The series has no trend and no structural breaks. The amplitude of the daily cycle grows slightly over the first half of the series and shrinks in the second half. This slow change in amplitude is the likely reason the KPSS test rejects stationarity.

This is the easiest of the five datasets and serves as a baseline.
