# M6864

- **Source:** M4 forecasting competition, series `M6864`
- **Frequency:** Monthly
- **Domain:** Macro
- **Start date (from M4-info):** 31-01-96 12:00
- **Length:** 2677 points (2659 from the M4 train file, 18 from the M4 test file)
- **Stationarity:** Non-stationary. The ADF test does not reject a unit root (p ≈ 1.0), and the KPSS test rejects stationarity (p = 0.01).
- **Regime change:** Change in behaviour, at t ≈ 2430

## Description

For most of its length, the series is smooth and low, with values between about 500 and 2,200. It falls in steps of piecewise-linear segments, with small level resets near t ≈ 800, 1200, 1600 and 2000. At t ≈ 2430 the level roughly triples to between 3,000 and 9,000, and large seasonal oscillations appear that the series did not have before.

The dynamics change, not only the level, so this is the clearest example of concept drift among the five datasets. The length is unusual for a monthly series, so the frequency label in M4 may not reflect the true sampling rate.
